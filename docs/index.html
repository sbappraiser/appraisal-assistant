How to

Step-by-step directions for each part of the Appraisal Assistant. Type a word to find the right section.

Start a new order
  1. On Home, click New order.
  2. 1 · Order: type the street address only (with the unit, e.g. #3), then Tab to City, State, ZIP and +4. City and ZIP are drop-down lists of the places you work (Santa Barbara County first, then Ventura County) – click and scroll with the mouse wheel. Not in the list? Pick Other – type it. Picking a ZIP fills the city, and Goleta, Carpinteria, Summerland or Montecito fill their ZIP; you can change either. When you upload the CRS report, it puts in the USPS form of the address with the ZIP+4. The file number fills in by your rule: the first 4 consonants of the street name (short names like Bath or Hill keep their first 4 letters), the house number, the unit if any, and the report count. 1729 Bath St → Bath1729-1; unit B → Bath1729-b-1; 223 W. Arrellaga St #3 → Arrl223-3-1. If you have done this property before in the assistant (or an old order is numbered the ACI way, like Bath1729a or Bath1729b), the count goes up: Bath1729-2. You can always type over it. Answer three questions: did the client send an engagement letter or will you write one, is it a purchase, and is the property listed now or in the last 12 months. Click Next.
  3. 2 · Documents: drop each file in its box – client’s engagement letter, CRS report, legal description, MLS listing with history, purchase contract with counters and addenda (as many PDFs as you have), preliminary title report, and anything else under Other documents. Each file is read as you add it, and checked against the property address: a file for another property is marked ⚠ and nothing is filled in from it.
  4. 3 · Check: see what was filled in. The client’s letter sets the client, loan number, borrower, due date, fee, report form, rent schedule, and AI use: a ban (for example “no AI without prior authorization”) sets No AI; confidentiality wording sets Yes – only with confidential information removed; nothing sets Yes. You can change any of it on the order.
  5. Click Read next to the prelim or contract to summarize it, then Open the order – or, if you are writing the letter, Next: write my engagement letter.
  6. The order saves by itself. To find it again, go to Home: Open an order lists your last 10 orders (click a row to open it); All files lets you search by file number, address and/or borrower – fill in any of the boxes and click Search (leave them empty to list every order).
  7. To delete an order from either list, click Delete at the end of its row, then click it again (it turns red and says Click again to delete) within 3 seconds. The order and all its uploaded documents are removed from this computer – make a Back up first if you might want it later.
Report form format and form
  1. Report form format: in New order step 1, click UAD 3.6 (Traditional), UAD 2.6 (Legacy), GPAR or Other. You can change it later in Scope and report on the order page.
  2. Form (just below it on the order page): the assistant picks the usual one for the property type and inspection – e.g. UAD 2.6 condo → Form 1073, exterior-only → Form 2055. Click it to choose another (1004, 1073, 1025, 2055, 1075, 1004C, 2090, 2095, 1004 Desktop, 1004D; URAR kinds for 3.6; GPAR forms), or Other – type it. Once you choose, it stays. It is the "Format" line in your engagement letter.
  3. Your choices are kept – the assistant does not change them, even if the client's letter names a different form. If you have not chosen, a letter that names a 1004 / 1073 / 1025 sets UAD 2.6 and that form.
Documents: upload once, kinds, search, sticky notes
  1. Uploading a file with the same name as one already in the order replaces it – you never get two copies. Its sticky notes and accepted entries stay. (Orders that already had a file twice are cleaned up when you open them.)
  2. Drop every file for the order into the Documents box, or click Choose files. Each one is sorted by kind; change the kind with its drop-down if it guessed wrong.
  3. Click a file name to open it. Click Read to open the reader (next section). Remove takes it out of the order.
  4. Search: type a word in "Search every document…" and press Enter or Search. It looks through the text of every document, your sticky notes and your accepted entries. Click a result to open that page with the word highlighted.
  5. Scanned PDFs have no text to search. Open the file in Acrobat → Scan & OCR → Recognize text, save, and drop it in again.
  6. Sticky notes are listed under Documents. Use the drop-down to show one tag (for example "Clear before transfer"). Click one to jump to its page.
Read a document: checklist, accept or reject, sticky notes
  1. In Documents, click Read. The page is on the left; the checklist for that kind of document is on the right. Use Previous / Next or the arrow keys to turn pages.
  2. Suggestions (from the text, or from Claude) appear in blue boxes with their page. Edit the words if needed, then Accept or Reject. Nothing goes into the order until you accept it.
  3. To add your own entry, read the page, type in "Type it yourself" and click Add. It keeps the page number.
  4. Sticky note: click + Sticky note on page …, pick a tag (Important, Affects market appeal, Clear before transfer, Ask the client, Put in the report, Follow up), type the note, Save note. Tick done when it is handled.
  5. Click Done to close the reader. Accepted entries are listed under Documents.
Preliminary title report
  1. Read the prelim. The date and the exceptions worth mentioning are suggested for you: easements, restrictions and CC&Rs, oil/gas/mineral items, encroachments, and items that may need to be cleared before a transfer. Deeds of trust, trust requirements, general printed items and paid taxes are left out (delinquent taxes are kept).
  2. Accept the ones you want. Under UAD 3.6 site – encumbrances, answer the easement, restriction, encroachment and mineral questions.
  3. Title summary for the report is drafted from what you accepted, with your title-expert statement. Decide how each easement or restriction affects market appeal, edit, then Copy and paste into SFREP.
Purchase contract packet
  1. Read the contract. C.A.R. forms print in scrambled text, so fill the checklist yourself while you read, or use Read with Claude on an AI order.
  2. Checklist: final negotiated price, acceptance date, personal property, leaseback, credits, buyer's broker (who pays, % or $), counters and addenda, loan and appraisal contingency, signing for an entity, court-ordered/estate/trust/atypical terms, written-in terms.
  3. Parts of this document: go to the first page of each part and click + Add a part; set its kind and pages. The sentence "I received a 75-page document which contains…" is built for you.
  4. Fill the UAD 3.6 sales contract boxes. 20.011 Sales contract analysis is drafted from your entries; edit, Copy, paste into SFREP.
Redact a document (make a redacted copy)
  1. In Documents, click Read on the file.
  2. On the right, find Redact a copy. Set the pages (all pages are filled in) and click Redact pages.
  3. Names from the order, emails, phone numbers, DocuSign IDs and loan/case/escrow numbers are blacked out for you. Type any other names (buyers, sellers, agents) in Also remove these names, separated by commas, then click outside the box.
  4. Choose Black box or Blur, then drag over signatures, initials, account numbers or anything else. Undo last box takes back the last one.
  5. Check every page, then click Save redacted PDF. It saves to Downloads (name ends in "REDACTED") and is added to the order's documents. The copy is pictures only, with no hidden text, so nothing removed can be recovered.
  6. This works on every order, including No AI orders. Nothing is sent anywhere.
Read with Claude, and redaction
  1. One time: get a Claude API key at platform.claude.com (the Claude Console) and paste it on Standard wording → AI settings, then Save AI settings.
  2. Only orders set to Yes or Yes – only with confidential information removed can send anything. On No AI orders nothing is ever sent.
  3. In Documents, click Read. At the top right is Remove confidential information, then send to Claude. Set the pages (up to 30 at a time) and click Redact pages.
  4. Names from the order, emails, phones, DocuSign IDs and loan/case/escrow numbers are blacked out for you. Type other names in Also remove these names. Pick Black box or Blur and drag over signatures, initials and anything else. Check every page.
  5. Click Send these … redacted pages to Claude. Only the redacted page pictures are sent – never the file itself.
  6. In about half a minute Claude’s findings appear as blue suggestions in the checklist, each with its page number. Click the page number to check it, edit if needed, then Accept or Reject. Only accepted entries go into the order and into the drafted summaries (title summary, contract analysis), which you copy into SFREP.
  7. Each send is recorded on the order (date, document, pages, redacted or not). On a Yes order you may also use Send without redacting.
Engagement letter
  1. On Order & letter, the letter preview on the left fills in from the order. Fill the scope, fee and delivery sections.
  2. The letter on the left is a preview. Type in the fields below it; the letter changes as you type. Clicking the preview takes you to the client fields.
  3. Client: start typing the name (or firm) – your saved clients pop up; pick one and the address, phone and email fill in. A new client is added to your list when you save.
  4. Access contact: type the name and pick Owner, Agent, Tenant or Other (Other lets you type it). Leave it blank and the letter says the Client is the contact.
  5. Delivery “Counted from” has Upon receipt of the signed agreement.
  6. Save as PDF (top right, and at the bottom of the fields): the print window opens; choose Destination Save as PDF and click Save. Untick “Headers and footers” under More settings the first time.
  7. Download Word letter (or Write engagement letter (Word) at the bottom) saves a Word file to Downloads, named by file number and street. Word opens downloaded files in Protected View; click Enable Editing in the yellow bar before you change or save it.
  8. The order saves itself while you type; “✓ Order saved” and the time show at the top and at the bottom.
  9. When the client sends their own engagement letter, drop it in Documents. It is checked for AI and confidentiality wording, and it shows on the left instead of your letter (My letter is set to “Not needed – client’s letter”). Click Read and list requirements to note the form, addenda, photo rules, due date, fee and inspection to-dos. Write my own letter instead switches back.
  10. Change the AI paragraph on Standard wording.
Market conditions
  1. Open the order, then the Market tab.
  2. Box 1 · Market Area Boundary (macro): in Flexmls search all residential listings inside your boundaries (actives, pendings and sales for the look-back period), export the CSV and drop it here. It is background for the Market Commentary and price trend.
  3. Box 2 · Search Criteria (micro): your search of the comparable properties within the boundaries – the same one you use for the 1004MC and, in a 2.6 report, the comparable listings and sales line at the top of the Sales Comparison Approach. Its results fill the UAD 3.6 Search Result Metrics (17.005–17.016), Demand/Supply and Marketing Time.
  4. In either table, untick a listing that does not belong (two units on one lot, a different market); click a column heading (List price, Sale price, $ / sq ft, DOM, Sq ft, dates) to sort, and again to reverse.
  5. Check the UAD 3.6 Market section in box 3. Pick Demand/Supply, Marketing Time and Distressed Market Competition (suggestions are shown). Type or pick the Market Area Boundary; Save this area to reuse it.
  6. Edit the search criteria and price trend comment, then Copy into SFREP.
  7. Save each graph with Save picture for the report.
  8. Once a month, drop the SBMLS Monthly MLS Statistics PDF in box 5 (South Coast trends). Type the Zillow figures for the city.
Comp genie from an order
  1. Click Comp genie on the order (or on its row in Orders). The comp genie opens with the subject filled in from the order.
  2. In the comp genie, From an order… lists your active orders.
  3. Use Chrome on this computer for both, so they share the same storage.
Adjustment grid and the full report for SFREP (Comp genie)
  1. On the iPad, export the Workfile package and save it to OneDrive. In the Comp genie, drop that .zip on the page: the subject column fills from your inspection (living area from your sketch, bedrooms, baths, quality and condition, site, garage, view…).
  2. Pick your comps with Use as 1, 2, 3… They line up in the Adjustment grid in UAD 3.6 order (sales and listing, site, dwelling, amenities).
  3. Type an adjustment in any cell (minus for downward). Or type a rate once beside a line – $ per sq ft, $ per usable acre, $ per bath, $ per garage space, % per month – and the empty cells use it (shown in grey). Clear a cell to go back to the rate.
  4. Usable Lot Area/Utility comes only from your archive or your own measurement. A comp without it says Measure it; type the subject's usable acres in the subject column if needed.
  5. Net and gross adjustments (gross over 25% turns red), adjusted prices, range, spread and median update as you type. Pick the weight for each comp and type your indicated value and comment.
  6. Full report for SFREP = your inspection + comps + grid + value in one .zip. SFREP comps file = comps and adjustments only. In SFREP: Home → Import Forms, into a copy of a report first, and tick Include adjustments.
Report details: assignment, legal, zoning, highest and best use, market (Comp genie)
  1. Open the Comp genie from the order (the Comp genie button on the order page), so it knows which order it belongs to.
  2. Below the grid, Report details shows what goes into the rest of the 3.6 report. Grey entries come from the order, your inspection or the CRS report; type over any of them.
  3. Assignment reason, file number, effective date, client, lender and AMC come from the order. Property valuation method is Traditional and "property data report used" is No unless you change them.
  4. Legal description, number of parcels (from the APNs), zoning code and all rights included. Type the zoning description once for a code (for example 7-R-1); it is remembered for every report with that code.
  5. Highest and best use: tick the four tests (or click All four tests met · present use), answer the present-use question, click Draft the comment and edit it.
  6. Market: comes from the order's Market tab here in the Appraisal Assistant: box 1 Market Area Boundary search (macro), box 2 Search Criteria search (micro – the Search Result Metrics), and once a month the SBMLS statistics PDF. Check the boundary, search criteria and Price Trend Analysis Commentary, then go back to the Comp genie.
  7. The sales contract answers come from the document reader (Read on the purchase contract).
  8. Type your opinion of market value, the value condition, the exposure time and a reconciliation comment. Then click Full report for SFREP.
  9. The usable lot area also goes into the Site Features comment as "Usable Lot Area/Utility: 0.18 ac." – it is not a site influence.
iPad: send orders, start an inspection, floor plan
  1. On the computer: Orders → Send active orders to the iPad. "Active Orders.json" saves to Downloads. Move it into your OneDrive folder.
  2. On the iPad: open the inspection app from its Home Screen icon, tap Get orders, pick Files → OneDrive → Active Orders.json.
  3. Tap Start inspection on the order. Address, county, year built, flood zone, permits, inspection date, access contact and your to-dos are filled in. Beds, baths and square feet are not.
  4. Floor plan: in the sketch, More → Floor plan underlay → "From the order".
Documents for a new order (step 2)
  1. Drop all the files at once in Drop everything here. Each file is sorted by what its first page says (CRS report, County permit history, prelim, contract, counters, MLS, plans …). The boxes below it are your checklist and show what arrived.
  2. You can still drop a file in a box. If its first page plainly says it is something else (for example a County “Permit History by Parcel” dropped in the prelim box), it is filed as what it really is and the status line tells you.
  3. Orders opened after this change are checked once, and a document filed under the wrong kind is corrected.
  4. To change a document's kind yourself: on the order page, under Documents, click the kind button next to the file. A small panel shows every kind at once; click the right one and the file is read again.
  5. Your own letter, signed and sent back, is filed as My engagement letter (signed) (it is recognized by its first page). It is not checked for AI wording, and the order's My letter is set to Signed.
Dictate your notes (iPad)
  1. In an inspection, tap the first tab, 🎤 Dictate. Tap in the box, then the microphone on the iPad keyboard, and talk.
  2. Say the area or room first, then what you see: exterior, garage, view, topography, site, landscaping, interior, heating / cooling / water heater, then each room – entry, living room, kitchen, family room, powder room, primary bedroom, primary bath, bedroom 2, hall bath, laundry room.
  3. Say “upstairs” or “level 2” before the rooms on that floor (“downstairs” or “main level” to go back).
  4. Ratings: “exterior quality Q4 condition C3”, “interior quality Q3 condition C2”, “overall quality Q4 condition C3”. Ceilings: “9 foot ceilings”. Counts: “2 story”, “2 car attached garage”, “island with seating”.
  5. Kitchen: “remodeled 5 to 10 years ago granite counters shaker cabinets Wolf range Sub-Zero refrigerator Bosch dishwasher”. Baths: “double sink freestanding tub separate shower frameless glass”, “tub shower combo tile surround”.
  6. Tap Read it. Each room shows as its own box (new, or an update to one you already have); untick a whole room or any line. Then tap Fill in the ticked items.
  7. You can dictate a room or two at a time. Words it did not understand are listed, never guessed. The dictation is kept in the Dictation log (workfile only).
Words to avoid (iPad)
  1. While you type or dictate a comment on the iPad, a red line under the box names any word reviewers treat as biased or subjective, such as mature, desirable, pride of ownership, exclusive or family-friendly. Reword it.
  2. The landscape choice "Mature Trees" is now Established Trees; inspections you already started are changed for you.
Back up, restore, new computer
  1. Click Back up (top right). One zip with every order, document, client and setting saves to Downloads. Keep it in OneDrive. Do this weekly.
  2. To bring everything to another computer or browser: open the Appraisal Assistant there in Chrome, click Restore, pick the zip.
  3. Your data lives in Chrome on this computer. Clearing Chrome's "site data" would erase it, so keep backups.
If something looks wrong
  1. Reload the page (Ctrl+R) to get the newest version.
  2. Use Chrome, not Edge or Firefox, and the same computer: each browser keeps its own data.
  3. "Not found in this browser" from the comp genie means the order is in a different browser.
  4. Send Claude a screenshot of what you see.

Every other clause comes word for word from your 2026 letters. The AI-use paragraph below is a draft for you to adjust; it prints at the end of the Confidentiality section.

Your orders, documents, clients and wording are kept in this browser on this computer only – nothing is sent anywhere. Press Back up now and then and keep the zip with your OneDrive records. To bring in the clients and orders from the old Engagement Letter Writer file: open it, press Back up there, then press Restore here and choose that file.

AI settings

Used only by Read with Claude in the document reader, and only on orders where AI use is Yes or Redacted. The key is kept in this browser on this computer. Get one at platform.claude.com (the Claude Console): add credits and a spending limit under Billing, then Settings → API keys → Create key.
