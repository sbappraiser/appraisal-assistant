How to

Step-by-step directions for each part of the Appraisal Assistant. Type a word to find the right section.

Start a new order
  1. Click Orders, then New order.
  2. Type the file number. Pick the assignment type. Lender and AMC orders are in the group Lender or AMC order – UAD 3.6 (purchase, refinance, HELOC, construction loan, other lending decision); for those, My letter is set to “Not needed – client’s letter”, and you can mark an FHA, VA or USDA loan under Lender and AMC.
  3. Drop the CRS report into Documents. The address, APN, owner, county, location and public-record facts fill in. Beds, baths and square feet are never filled in for the subject.
  4. Fill in the client (or Pick a saved client), lender or AMC if any, inspection date and time, access contact, fee.
  5. Choose AI use on this job: Yes, Yes with confidential information removed, or No AI.
  6. The order saves by itself while you type. Save order also adds a new client to your client list.
Documents: upload once, kinds, search, sticky notes
  1. Drop every file for the order into the Documents box, or click Choose files. Each one is sorted by kind; change the kind with its drop-down if it guessed wrong.
  2. Click a file name to open it. Click Read to open the reader (next section). Remove takes it out of the order.
  3. Search: type a word in "Search every document…" and press Enter or Search. It looks through the text of every document, your sticky notes and your accepted entries. Click a result to open that page with the word highlighted.
  4. Scanned PDFs have no text to search. Open the file in Acrobat → Scan & OCR → Recognize text, save, and drop it in again.
  5. Sticky notes are listed under Documents. Use the drop-down to show one tag (for example "Clear before transfer"). Click one to jump to its page.
Read a document: checklist, accept or reject, sticky notes
  1. In Documents, click Read. The page is on the left; the checklist for that kind of document is on the right. Use Previous / Next or the arrow keys to turn pages.
  2. Suggestions (from the text, or from Claude) appear in blue boxes with their page. Edit the words if needed, then Accept or Reject. Nothing goes into the order until you accept it.
  3. To add your own entry, read the page, type in "Type it yourself" and click Add. It keeps the page number.
  4. Sticky note: click + Sticky note on page …, pick a tag (Important, Affects market appeal, Clear before transfer, Ask the client, Put in the report, Follow up), type the note, Save note. Tick done when it is handled.
  5. Click Done to close the reader. Accepted entries are listed under Documents.
Preliminary title report
  1. Read the prelim. The date and the exceptions worth mentioning are suggested for you: easements, restrictions and CC&Rs, oil/gas/mineral items, encroachments, and items that may need to be cleared before a transfer. Deeds of trust, trust requirements, general printed items and paid taxes are left out (delinquent taxes are kept).
  2. Accept the ones you want. Under UAD 3.6 site – encumbrances, answer the easement, restriction, encroachment and mineral questions.
  3. Title summary for the report is drafted from what you accepted, with your title-expert statement. Decide how each easement or restriction affects market appeal, edit, then Copy and paste into SFREP.
Purchase contract packet
  1. Read the contract. C.A.R. forms print in scrambled text, so fill the checklist yourself while you read, or use Read with Claude on an AI order.
  2. Checklist: final negotiated price, acceptance date, personal property, leaseback, credits, buyer's broker (who pays, % or $), counters and addenda, loan and appraisal contingency, signing for an entity, court-ordered/estate/trust/atypical terms, written-in terms.
  3. Parts of this document: go to the first page of each part and click + Add a part; set its kind and pages. The sentence "I received a 75-page document which contains…" is built for you.
  4. Fill the UAD 3.6 sales contract boxes. 20.011 Sales contract analysis is drafted from your entries; edit, Copy, paste into SFREP.
Redact a document (make a redacted copy)
  1. In Documents, click Read on the file.
  2. On the right, find Redact a copy. Set the pages (all pages are filled in) and click Redact pages.
  3. Names from the order, emails, phone numbers, DocuSign IDs and loan/case/escrow numbers are blacked out for you. Type any other names (buyers, sellers, agents) in Also remove these names, separated by commas, then click outside the box.
  4. Choose Black box or Blur, then drag over signatures, initials, account numbers or anything else. Undo last box takes back the last one.
  5. Check every page, then click Save redacted PDF. It saves to Downloads (name ends in "REDACTED") and is added to the order's documents. The copy is pictures only, with no hidden text, so nothing removed can be recovered.
  6. This works on every order, including No AI orders. Nothing is sent anywhere.
Read with Claude, and redaction
  1. One time: get a Claude API key at platform.claude.com (the Claude Console) and paste it on Standard wording → AI settings, then Save AI settings.
  2. Only orders set to Yes or Yes – only with confidential information removed can send anything. On No AI orders nothing is ever sent.
  3. In Documents, click Read. At the top right is Remove confidential information, then send to Claude. Set the pages (up to 30 at a time) and click Redact pages.
  4. Names from the order, emails, phones, DocuSign IDs and loan/case/escrow numbers are blacked out for you. Type other names in Also remove these names. Pick Black box or Blur and drag over signatures, initials and anything else. Check every page.
  5. Click Send these … redacted pages to Claude. Only the redacted page pictures are sent – never the file itself.
  6. In about half a minute Claude’s findings appear as blue suggestions in the checklist, each with its page number. Click the page number to check it, edit if needed, then Accept or Reject. Only accepted entries go into the order and into the drafted summaries (title summary, contract analysis), which you copy into SFREP.
  7. Each send is recorded on the order (date, document, pages, redacted or not). On a Yes order you may also use Send without redacting.
Engagement letter
  1. On Order & letter, the letter preview on the right fills in from the order. Fill the scope, fee and delivery sections.
  2. Click Download Word letter; it saves to Downloads, named by file number and street. Send it as you do now.
  3. When the client sends their own engagement letter, drop it in Documents. It is checked for AI and confidentiality wording, and it shows on the right instead of your letter (My letter is set to “Not needed – client’s letter”). Click Read and list requirements to note the form, addenda, photo rules, due date, fee and inspection to-dos. Write my own letter instead switches back.
  4. Change the AI paragraph on Standard wording.
Market conditions
  1. Open the order, then the Market tab.
  2. In Flexmls, search the market area (actives, pendings and sales for the look-back period), export as CSV, and drop it in box 1. Untick listings that are not competitive.
  3. Check the UAD 3.6 fields in box 2. Pick demand/supply, marketing time and distressed competition (suggestions are shown). Type or pick the market-area boundary; Save this area to reuse it.
  4. Edit the search criteria and price trend comment, then Copy into SFREP.
  5. Save each graph with Save picture for the report.
  6. Once a month, drop the SBMLS Monthly MLS Statistics PDF in box 4. Type the Zillow figures for the city.
Comp genie from an order
  1. Click Comp genie on the order (or on its row in Orders). The comp genie opens with the subject filled in from the order.
  2. In the comp genie, From an order… lists your active orders.
  3. Use Chrome on this computer for both, so they share the same storage.
iPad: send orders, start an inspection, floor plan
  1. On the computer: Orders → Send active orders to the iPad. "Active Orders.json" saves to Downloads. Move it into your OneDrive folder.
  2. On the iPad: open the inspection app from its Home Screen icon, tap Get orders, pick Files → OneDrive → Active Orders.json.
  3. Tap Start inspection on the order. Address, county, year built, flood zone, permits, inspection date, access contact and your to-dos are filled in. Beds, baths and square feet are not.
  4. Floor plan: in the sketch, More → Floor plan underlay → "From the order".
Back up, restore, new computer
  1. Click Back up (top right). One zip with every order, document, client and setting saves to Downloads. Keep it in OneDrive. Do this weekly.
  2. To bring everything to another computer or browser: open the Appraisal Assistant there in Chrome, click Restore, pick the zip.
  3. Your data lives in Chrome on this computer. Clearing Chrome's "site data" would erase it, so keep backups.
If something looks wrong
  1. Reload the page (Ctrl+R) to get the newest version.
  2. Use Chrome, not Edge or Firefox, and the same computer: each browser keeps its own data.
  3. "Not found in this browser" from the comp genie means the order is in a different browser.
  4. Send Claude a screenshot of what you see.
